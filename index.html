1. Introduction

Lame Lobster Studio ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Nitro Clash ("the Game") may collect, use, and share information when you play the Game.

2. Information We Collect

A. Guest Profile Information

Nitro Clash currently allows players to play as guests without creating an account. Players may enter a nickname used to identify them during multiplayer gameplay. This nickname may be transmitted through Photon services and displayed to other players in multiplayer rooms.

The Game does not currently require an email address, phone number, Google account, or Facebook account to play.

B. Device and Advertising Information

Our advertising provider, Appodeal, and its advertising mediation partners may automatically collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, device model, operating system, network information, and information about ad impressions, views, and interactions. The information processed depends on the advertising partners and the settings and permissions applicable to your device.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services. We do not intentionally use the Game to collect precise GPS location.

C. Multiplayer Information

Nitro Clash uses Photon PUN 2 to provide online multiplayer functionality. Photon may process information needed to establish and maintain multiplayer connections, such as IP address, player identifiers, nicknames, and session or connection information. Your nickname and relevant gameplay information may be visible to other players in the same multiplayer room.

Photon Privacy Policy: https://www.photonengine.com/terms/privacyandcookiepolicy

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide and maintain game functionality, enable multiplayer gameplay, display and measure advertisements, provide optional rewarded advertisements, prevent fraud and misuse, maintain security, and improve the Game.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game uses third-party services that may process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Appodeal: advertising mediation and ad delivery. Privacy Policy: https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy/
  • Photon Engine: online multiplayer networking. Privacy Policy: https://www.photonengine.com/terms/privacyandcookiepolicy

These providers may share information with their service providers, advertising partners, or other relevant third parties as necessary to provide their services. Their independent practices are governed by their own policies.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

As of the effective date above, the Game does not use a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. If we add such services, we will update this policy to describe the relevant data processing.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information that players directly provide to us. Information may be processed by third-party providers when necessary to operate multiplayer services, deliver advertisements, measure advertising, prevent fraud, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Retention

We retain information under our control only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Information processed by third-party providers is subject to their respective retention policies.

8. Children's Privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us using the email below.

9. Data Security
